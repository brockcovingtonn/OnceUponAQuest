# Once Upon a Quest — Privacy Policy

_Effective October 15, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains what information Higher Thinking Consulting ("we", "us") collects through the Once Upon a Quest mobile app (the "App"), how we use it, who can see it, and the choices you have. We collect only what the App needs to work. We do not sell your personal information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

## 1. Information you give us

- Email address — to send your sign-in code and important account notices.
- Username and optional display name.
- Your explorer avatar — the cartoon look you build (skin tone, hair, eyes, freckles) and the outfit items you wear — or a profile photo you choose from your photo library.
- Age confirmation (that you are 13 or older) and the date you accepted the Terms.
- Check-ins — which spots you found and when. Typed answers are checked and then discarded; we do not store them.
- Check-in photos you take for photo spots, and optional snack photos.
- Snack Passport entries, problem reports (with any note you add), friend requests and friendships.
- Reports you make about other users (with any note you add) and the people you block.
- Anything you send us by email, such as feedback or support requests.

## 2. Information collected when you use the App

- Precise location — only if you allow location access, and only while the App is open: at the moment you tap Check in or stamp a Storybook Lens page at a place that has a location pin (sent to our server to confirm you are nearby, and not stored), while the Storybook Lens is open (used on your phone to show which landmarks are around you), and while you turn on "Show me" on the Map (used on your phone to place your explorer on the map). Location used by the Lens and the Map is never sent to us, and your position is never shown to anyone else. The App never uses location in the background.
- Camera — when you tap a button to take a photo, and while the Storybook Lens is open to show a live view. The Lens view is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
- Compass direction — while the Storybook Lens is open, to show which landmarks you are facing, and while "Show me" is on in the Map, to point your explorer's arrow. It stays on your phone.
- Wishing Stars and wardrobe — the stars you earn and spend, and the outfit items you own. Only you can see your stars and wardrobe; others see only what your explorer is wearing.
- Storybook Lens stamps — which landmark stories you have stamped, and when.
- Saved quests — quests you save for your next visit. Only you can see them.
- Photo library — only through your phone's system picker, and only for the single photo you choose as your profile photo.
- On your device only — your sign-in session (kept in your phone's secure storage), your Parade mode setting, a copy of quest content and your progress so the App works without signal, and check-ins (including photos) made offline until they upload. Signing out clears the copy and any check-ins waiting to upload.
- Server logs — our hosting provider records basic technical data such as IP address, time of request and error messages, to keep the service secure and working.
- Error reports — if the App hits an error or crashes, it sends us a short report (the error message and code location, the screen, the app version and update, and your phone's platform and system version), linked to your account if you are signed in. We use these only to fix bugs and delete them after 90 days.
- We do not use advertising identifiers, third-party analytics, or tracking pixels.

## 3. How we use information

- To create and secure your account and sign you in.
- To run the game: verify check-ins, award stamps and badges, and show your progress.
- To power Friends and the leaderboard.
- To keep people safe: review problem reports, enforce our Terms, and prevent abuse or cheating.
- To improve quest content and fix bugs, and to measure in aggregate how quests are used (for example, how many explorers finish a quest on their first visit).
- To comply with the law and respond to lawful requests.

## 4. Who can see your information

- Any signed-in user: your username, display name, explorer avatar (including what it is wearing) and profile photo if you chose one (for example, when they search for your username or see a friend request).
- Friends you accept: which spots you have found and when (including Snack Passport entries), your Storybook Lens stamps, and your badges. Friends cannot see your check-in photos.
- Only you: your check-in photos and your email address.
- Only you: the list of people you have blocked. People you block are not told.
- Only us: problem reports, reports about users (the person reported is never told who reported them), server logs, and your email address. We access account data only as needed to operate, secure, moderate and support the App.

## 5. Service providers and other sharing

We share information only in these cases:

- Service providers who process data for us under contract: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage, hosted in the United States (West Coast)), our email delivery provider (to send sign-in codes), and Apple and Google (to distribute the App).
- When required by law, or to protect the safety, rights or property of our users, the public, or us.
- As part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this Policy will continue to apply to your information.
- With your permission.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under California law.

## 6. Children and teens

The App is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. During sign-up, anyone who says they are under 13 is stopped, and the sign-in account that was just created is deleted. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us at higherthinkingconsulting@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Teens aged 13 to 17 should use the App with a parent or guardian's permission. Profiles are visible to other signed-in users by username, so we encourage teens to use a username and profile photo that do not reveal their real name or face, and to friend only people they know.

## 7. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account (Profile → Delete my account), we immediately delete your profile (including your explorer avatar), check-ins, Storybook Lens stamps, Wishing Stars, wardrobe items, saved quests, badges, friendships, blocks, problem reports, reports you made or that were made about you, check-in photos and profile photo. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for a limited time (typically no more than 30 days) before they are overwritten. Server logs are kept for a short period set by our hosting provider. We may keep information longer if the law requires it.

## 8. Your choices and rights

- Update your display name and avatar any time in Profile → Edit profile.
- Delete your account and data any time in Profile → Delete my account.
- Turn location or camera access on or off in your phone's settings. The App works without them, but location-pinned and photo spots cannot be checked in, location-pinned Lens pages cannot be stamped, and the Storybook Lens shows a storybook picture instead of the camera.
- Ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing higherthinkingconsulting@gmail.com. We will verify your request using your account email.

## 9. California privacy rights

If you live in California, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to request access to, correction of, and deletion of your personal information; to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do neither); and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. The only sensitive information we use is precise location, and only to confirm a check-in or Lens stamp at your request and, on your phone, to show nearby landmarks in the Storybook Lens. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. You may use an authorized agent, who must provide proof of your permission. Send requests to higherthinkingconsulting@gmail.com.

## 10. Visitors from outside the United States

The App is intended for use in the United States, and information is stored in the United States (West Coast). If you use it from elsewhere, you understand your information will be processed in the United States. Where laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on performing our contract with you (the Terms) and our legitimate interest in keeping the App safe, and you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

## 11. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit (HTTPS), private storage for photos, database rules that limit each user to their own data, answer keys that never leave our server, and sign-in sessions kept in your phone's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your email account secure and contact us if you notice anything unusual.

## 12. Do Not Track

The App does not track you across other apps or websites, so we do not respond differently to Do Not Track signals.

## 13. Changes to this Policy

We will update this Policy when our practices change. If the changes are material, we will tell you in the App before they take effect. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated.
